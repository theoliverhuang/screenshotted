Our Work

The Qu Health Guide (healthguide.lgbt) tackles the lack of queer inclusive sexual health education by creating resources for young adults and educational institutions

For Individuals

Through social media, Qu Health Guide has run campaigns promoting safe sex & STI testing. Our website, healthguide.lgbt provides information on sexual health.

For Colleges and Universities

We create posters and materials for LGBTQ+ centers, student health centers, & student clubs to educate fellow students on queer health
