The Fellowship

A 3-month program empowering high school and college students to understand research on screens and advocate for better screen policies in their community

Apply

Over four months a small cohort of high school and college students will:

  • Learn how to actionably engage with research on youth mental health and digital wellbeing
  • Collaborate with mentors, guest speakers, and other youth leaders to translate research into real-world change
  • Design an “action project” to create evidence-based impact in their schools, communities, or local/national governments
